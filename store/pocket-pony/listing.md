## Short description (80 characters max)

Raise a gentle little horse. No ads, no rushing, and your pony never dies.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

A little horse of your very own, to raise from the day it's born.

A mother horse is waiting in the paddock, and soon her foal arrives. It's shy at first and hides behind the apple tree, but feed it, brush it and play with it, and it learns to trust you. Look after it well and it grows up from a wobbly foal into a fine horse, with a personality all its own.

LOOK AFTER IT
• Feed it apples, carrots and hay
• Brush off the mud, keep it clean and see the vet when it's poorly
• Turn the barn light off at bedtime so it sleeps well
• Boop its nose, tickle its tummy and pat its head: it follows your finger with its eyes

FIVE GENTLE GAMES
• Grooming: brush, pick the stones from its hooves, comb out its mane and choose a ribbon
• Hide and Seek, Apple Catch, Blurry Photo and Quick Catch
• No timers to beat in the calm games, and no high scores to worry about

A LIVING FARM
• Real time: it gets hungry, sleepy and playful through the day, and grows over days
• Hens and chicks, a goat, a sheep, the farm dog, a piglet, the barn cat, bunnies, deer and an owl stop by to visit
• Seasons and holidays: snow in winter, a tree at Christmas, a pumpkin at Halloween
• Shake the apple tree and watch your pony trot over for a crunchy snack
• Collect 24 photos for your pony's album

GENTLE BY DESIGN
• Your pony never dies. If it's left alone too long it wanders off, and you can always coax it back
• Away for a few days? Leave it at the boarding stable and it waits for you, fed and happy
• Big buttons and no fast tapping, so anyone can play
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no internet needed. Everything stays on your phone

Pocket Pony is made by one person, as a gift for a horse-loving niece. We hope your family enjoys it too.
