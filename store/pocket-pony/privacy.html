Pocket Pony: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Pocket Pony is a virtual pet game for Android, made by Blue Beetle Games. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. Pocket Pony does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information, from children or from anyone else. There are no accounts or sign-ins, and no names, email addresses, photos, contacts or location are asked for or collected.

What stays on your phone

Your pony's progress (its name, how it's doing, its photo album) and your settings (brightness, sound, screen timeout) are saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If Android's own device backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your personal Google backup, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, and we never see it.

Internet, ads and tracking

Pocket Pony does not use the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services that collect data. There are no in-app purchases.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can remind you when your pony needs you (for example, when it's hungry). These reminders are worked out and shown on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

Children

Pocket Pony is designed for children and families. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not collect any from children under 13. It meets the requirements of Google Play's Families policy and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Links

The only link out of the app is the one to this page, in Settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever started collecting information, we would update this policy and the app's Google Play data safety section first.
